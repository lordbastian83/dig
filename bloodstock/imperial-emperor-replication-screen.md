# The Imperial Emperor Replication Screen — ~£60k budget

**Prepared:** 26 July 2026 · Companion to `investment-analysis-2026-07.md`

## What Imperial Emperor actually is (calibration, not legend)

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Pedigree | Dubawi (elite dirt-translating sire) × Zhukova (Fastnet Rock), Group 1-winning dam |
| Origin | Godolphin homebred, originally trained by Charlie Appleby |
| Acquisition | **$300,000**, ERA Racing in Dubai Sale, March 2024 — bought by Deva Racing, sent to Bhupat Seemar |
| Outcome | Al Maktoum Classic (G2) and Al Maktoum Mile (G2) 2025, **Al Maktoum Challenge (G1) 2026**, Dubai World Cup entry |

The trade that worked: an elite-pedigree Godolphin cast-off whose European
form understated him, re-routed to Meydan dirt where the Dubawi/Street Cry-line
pedigree translates and where prize money per start is a multiple of the UK's.
He was **not** a £20-30k bargain — the replication at ~£60k is the same
archetype bought one tier earlier: before the UK form makes the dirt upside
obvious to everyone else.

**Governance warning (directly relevant to syndication structure):** Deva
Racing was subsequently barred by British racing over unpaid prize money,
having reportedly sold shares in the horse beyond 100% and entered him in
sales without members' consent. The horse was a triumph; the vehicle was not.
Any GTS-style structure must have single-signatory-proof controls on entries,
sales and share issuance.

## The screen (hard filters — all must pass)

1. **Breeder/vendor:** Godolphin, Juddmonte, Shadwell or equivalent powerhouse
   draft — a genuine cast-off, not a horse being traded because it's exposed.
2. **Sire:** proven dirt/Tapeta-translating line — Dubawi, Night of Thunder,
   Too Darn Hot, New Bay, Blue Point, or damsire from the Street Cry /
   Shamardal / Medaglia d'Oro influence cluster.
3. **Dam:** black-type producer or Group-performed herself (first two dams
   must carry black type — non-negotiable, this is the residual-value floor).
4. **Age/exposure:** 3yo (or lightly raced 4yo), **≤ 7 career starts**,
   official rating **85–95** — high enough to prove ability, low enough that
   the market hasn't repriced the ceiling.
5. **Surface signal:** at least one win or clear best-figure on all-weather
   (Newcastle/Kempton Tapeta-Polytrack) — the cheapest available proxy for
   dirt translation.
6. **Physical:** strong-topped, correct forelimbs, no wind-surgery history;
   full vet file at inspection or the mandatory −20% discount applies.

**Budget frame:** "around £60k" ⇒ test whether a hard limit near 56,000 gns
(≈ £58,800) clears the EV cap.

**Campaign model** (12 months: UK autumn prep → Dubai Carnival Nov–Mar, or
UK all-weather winter with sale-to-UAE exit after a win):

Costs: UK training 4 months (£11,000) + flight/export to UAE (£8,000) + UAE
season costs net of owner incentives (£11,000) ≈ **£30,000**.

Expected inflows for a screened lot (rated 85–95, dirt-pedigree, powerhouse
cast-off):

- Prize-money EV, 5–6 Carnival/UK-AW starts: **£25,000** (Meydan handicap
  purses make even a mid-table campaign cash-relevant).
- Residual tree:
  - 10% Carnival group-class breakthrough (the Imperial Emperor branch) → £350,000 → £35,000
  - 20% Meydan/UK winner, reprices to 100+ → £120,000 → £24,000
  - 40% competitive mid-tier → £30,000 → £12,000
  - 30% fails to translate → £8,000 → £2,400
  - **E[residual] = £73,400**
- **Total E[inflows] = £98,400**

Breakeven cap = 98,400 − 30,000 = **£68,400**
Margin-adjusted (÷1.15) = **£59,478** → after 5% buyer frictions ⇒

## **HARD MAX BID: 56,000 gns** (≈ £58,800)
### If vet file incomplete at the fall of the hammer: **45,000 gns** (mandatory −20%)

Sensitivity: the bid only breaks if P(group-class branch) < ~4% — i.e. the
screen's job is to keep that branch's probability real. A lot that passes
filters 1–3 but is rated 96+ will cost 150,000+ gns and fails the cap; a lot
under 85 fails the proof-of-ability filter. The 85–95 band **is** the trade.

</quantitative_reasoning>

## Execution notes

- This is a hard limit order. The Autumn HIT market will be hot (July Sale
  median +36% YoY) and Godolphin drafts attract UAE money for exactly this
  profile — expect to be outbid on the obvious ones. The screen wins on the
  lot the ring under-reads, not the lot everyone can see.
- Action on catalogue release (~early Oct): run all 6 filters across the full
  catalogue, shortlist ≤ 8 lots, order vet files, inspect, bid to limit.
- One draw from the tree = 30% total-loss-adjacent branch. Size the syndicate
  accordingly and disclose it.

## Why no named candidates today

There is no live catalogue on 26 July 2026: the July Sale's 564 lots are sold,
and the Autumn HIT / Arqana Autumn catalogues publish in October. Naming
"buys" now would be curation, not computation. The pipeline instead:

1. **~6 Oct** — Tattersalls Autumn HIT catalogue drops → screen all lots
   (CSV import → instant scoring), shortlist ≤8, order vet files.
2. **~early Nov** — Arqana Autumn catalogue drops → same screen, PSF-aware.
3. Between now and then — screen each Tattersalls Online monthly catalogue
   (small, minutes of work, occasional mispriced cast-off).

## Market intelligence worth holding

- Godolphin's July draft was led by Renaissance Lady (unraced Pinatubo 3yo,
  500,000 gns) and Wild Angel (Too Darn Hot, 400,000 gns) — both to Ace
  Stud. The market pays up for the *obvious* pedigree; the screen's rating
  band (85–95) deliberately sits below where that money concentrates.
- The Tattersalls Online ERA Sale traded £1.97m in its last renewal —
  the Dubai exit market is liquid and growing. That is the residual-value
  branch of the EV model getting stronger, not weaker.

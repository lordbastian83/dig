Sales calendar

The 6-filter shopping list
  1. Powerhouse cast-off — Godolphin / Juddmonte / Shadwell draft
  2. Dirt-translating sire line — Dubawi, Night Of Thunder, Too Darn Hot, New Bay, Blue Point; or Street Cry / Shamardal / Medaglia d'Oro damsire
  3. Black type in the first two dams — the residual-value floor
  4. Official rating 85–95 — proven ability, unpriced ceiling
  5. ≤ 7 career starts — unexposed, still progressive
  6. All-weather form — the cheapest dirt proxy (French PSF counts)

Full reasoning: bloodstock/imperial-emperor-replication-screen.md. Data feeds and automation: bloodstock/DATA.md.

Valuation model (editable)

Max bid = (prize EV + probability-weighted residual − costs) ÷ margin ÷ frictions, −20% without a clean vet file, capped at budget. A perfect-score lot lands on the committed 56,000 gns limit.

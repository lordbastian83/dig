Valuation model (editable)

Max bid = (prize EV + probability-weighted residual − campaign costs) ÷ (1 + margin) ÷ 1.05 frictions→gns, then −20% if the vet file isn't clean, floored to the nearest 1,000 gns and capped at budget. The upside-branch probability scales from 4% to 10% with the lot's quality score — a perfect-score lot reproduces the committed 56,000 gns limit exactly.

Model assumptions are stated, editable and committed to the repo so they can be challenged — this is analysis, not financial advice.
